EXERCISE PICTURES
=================

Put a picture in this folder and it appears at the top of that exercise's
"How to do it" panel in the app — the one that opens when you tap an exercise
name mid-session.

You do not need to change any code. Just name the file correctly and put it
here.


HOW TO ADD ONE
--------------

1. Get a picture or a short animation of the movement.
2. Rename it to exactly match the name in the list below, keeping the dashes
   and all lower case.
3. Drop it in this folder.
4. Push the change (git push). It is live a minute later.

Example: a clip of a goblet squat becomes    goblet-squat.mp4
         a photo of one becomes              goblet-squat.jpg


FILE TYPES
----------

Video:    .mp4   .webm          <-- best option
Stills:   .jpg   .jpeg   .png   .webp   .gif

A SHORT VIDEO IS THE BEST CHOICE. These are movements, not positions, and a
clip shows the whole rep. It plays automatically, silently, on a loop — it
behaves like an animation rather than a video you have to operate.

Use .mp4. iPhones record .mov files, which many Android browsers will not
play, so convert those to .mp4 first.

Two to five seconds is plenty. One clean rep, looping.

If you add both a video and a photo for the same exercise, the video is used
and the photo becomes its holding image while the clip loads.

A .gif also works but is a poor choice for video — the same clip as a .gif is
often ten times the size and looks worse.


HOW LONG AND HOW BIG
--------------------

Aim for:

    video   2-5 seconds, under about 1 MB each
    photo   under about 300 KB each

These get downloaded onto the phone so they work with no internet. Nineteen
clips at 1 MB is about 19 MB, which is fine. Nineteen clips straight off a
phone camera could be 400 MB, which is not — the app would take an age to
install.

To shrink a clip, most phones can trim it, and any free converter can drop the
resolution to 720p. It is being watched on a phone screen, so 720p is plenty.

YOU DO NOT HAVE TO DO ALL OF THEM
---------------------------------

Add one, or five, or all nineteen. Any exercise without a picture simply shows
the written cue as it does now — nothing breaks and nothing looks unfinished.


IF YOU ARE FILMING THEM YOURSELF
-------------------------------

Film from the side, in decent light, with the whole body in frame, and capture
one full rep at normal speed rather than the hardest point.

Filming her own sessions is worth more than any stock clip: the bench is her
bench, the dumbbells are her dumbbells, and the room is her room.


A NOTE ON WHERE THEY COME FROM
------------------------------

Do not use pictures pulled from someone else's website or app. Either take
them yourself, or use a source that clearly permits reuse. It is one thing to
link to a YouTube search, which the app already does, and another to copy
someone's photographs into your own app.
