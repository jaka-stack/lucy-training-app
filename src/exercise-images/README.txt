EXERCISE PICTURES
=================

Put a picture in this folder and it appears at the top of that exercise's
"How to do it" panel in the app — the one that opens when you tap an exercise
name mid-session.

You do not need to change any code. Just name the file correctly and put it
here.


HOW TO ADD ONE
--------------

1. Get a picture or a short animation of the movement.
2. Rename it to exactly match the name in the list below, keeping the dashes
   and all lower case.
3. Drop it in this folder.
4. Push the change (git push). It is live a minute later.

Example: a photo of a goblet squat becomes    goblet-squat.jpg


FILE TYPES
----------

Any of these work:   .jpg   .jpeg   .png   .webp   .gif

An animated .gif is often better than a still photo, because these are
movements, not positions. A two or three second loop showing the whole rep is
ideal.


THE FILENAMES

    db-rdl                    (the Romanian deadlift)

    chest-supported-row

    rear-delt-fly
    biceps-curl
    triceps-extension
    front-plank


YOU DO NOT HAVE TO DO ALL OF THEM
---------------------------------

Add one, or five, or all nineteen. Any exercise without a picture simply shows
the written cue as it does now — nothing breaks and nothing looks unfinished.


KEEP THE FILES SMALL
--------------------

These get downloaded onto the phone so they work with no internet. Aim for
under about 300 KB each; a few hundred kilobytes is plenty for something
viewed on a phone. A 5 MB photo straight off a camera will make the app slow
to install and is no clearer.

If you are taking them yourself: film from the side, in decent light, with the
whole body in frame, and show the full movement rather than the hardest point.


A NOTE ON WHERE THEY COME FROM
------------------------------

Do not use pictures pulled from someone else's website or app. Either take
them yourself, or use a source that clearly permits reuse. It is one thing to
link to a YouTube search, which the app already does, and another to copy
someone's photographs into your own app.
